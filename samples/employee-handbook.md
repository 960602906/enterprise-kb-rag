## Paid Time Off (PTO) / 带薪休假

### Overview / 概述

Full-time employees accrue **15 days** of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year.
全日制员工每个日历年累计 **15 天** 带薪休假（PTO）。

PTO may be used for vacation, personal errands, or short-term personal illness
(when sick leave is exhausted).
PTO 可用于年假、个人事务，或在病假用尽后用于短期个人疾病。

### Accrual / 累计规则

- Accrual starts on the hire date / 自入职日起累计
- Accrual rate: **1.25 days per month** / 每月 **1.25 天**
- Maximum bank: **20 days** carry-over into the next year / 最多结转 **20 天** 至下一年度
- Unused PTO above the carry-over cap is forfeited on Dec 31 /
  超过结转上限的未使用 PTO 于 12 月 31 日作废

### Requesting leave / 请假流程

1. Submit a request in the HR portal at least **5 business days** before the start date /
   至少提前 **5 个工作日** 在 HR 门户提交申请
2. Manager approval is required for requests of **3+ consecutive days** /
   连续 **3 天及以上** 须经直属经理批准
3. Emergency leave may be requested same-day with a follow-up note within 24 hours /
   紧急请假可当日申请，并在 24 小时内补交说明

### Blackout periods / 冻结期

PTO is generally not approved during:
以下期间通常不批准 PTO：

- Company fiscal year-end close (last 5 business days of March) /
  财年结账（三月最后 5 个工作日）
- Scheduled product launch weeks (announced quarterly) /
  已公布的产品发布周（按季度通知）

Exceptions require VP-level approval.
例外须经副总裁级批准。

### Sick leave / 病假

Separate from PTO, employees receive **10 sick days** per year.
除 PTO 外，员工每年另有 **10 天** 病假。

A doctor's note is required for absences longer than **3 consecutive days**.
连续缺勤超过 **3 天** 须提供医生证明。
